About us

About Kitefern Press

A kite for play. A fern for the quiet kind of growth. Kitefern Press makes books for both.

Kitefern Press is a small, independent publisher of practical, good-looking paperbacks for the people in your life: a parent who starts each day with a puzzle, a grandparent with stories to tell, a kid on a long car ride, and a family sharing the work of care.

We keep things simple. Every book is designed to be clear, calm and useful from the first page to the last.

  • i.

    Clear before clever. Big type, generous spacing and layouts that are easy on the eyes.

  • ii.

    Nothing left hanging. Every puzzle and maze book comes with a full answer key.

  • iii.

    Printed on demand. Each copy is printed when it's ordered, so there are no warehouses of unsold books.
